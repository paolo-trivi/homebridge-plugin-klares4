## Changelog entries (2.3.0)

### Fixed
- A malformed entry in a panel frame, or an error while handling one device, no longer drops the other devices of the same frame, at discovery or in status updates. The error is logged with the frame type, the device id and a stack trace instead of a generic "Message parsing error". (WS-03, TD-T-16)
- HomeKit, MQTT and Matter are handled independently for each device: a HomeKit error no longer keeps a device from being registered on Matter or published on MQTT. (PLAT-02)
- Bridges with more than 149 devices: Homebridge's HomeKit bridge refuses accessories beyond 149. The plugin now logs one clear warning, and the devices beyond the limit are still exposed on Matter and published on MQTT instead of being lost together with every device discovered after them. (PLAT-02)
- The state of a device excluded with `excludeZones`, `excludeOutputs`, `excludeSensors` or `excludeScenarios` is no longer published on MQTT. (PLAT-06, SP-09)
- A Homebridge shutdown during startup no longer lets the plugin connect and log in to the panel afterwards. (PLAT-09)
- An error in the connection callbacks no longer leaves the session without heartbeat and without reading the panel data; an error in a raw-message listener (debug capture) no longer crashes the bridge. (WS-03)
- Reconnections: each login is now a discovery sync of its own. A reconnect whose discovery read fails can no longer count towards removing accessories, and a device deleted on the panel is removed from HomeKit and Matter after three complete syncs, also without restarting Homebridge. (PLAT-04, MAT-02 for endpoints registered in the session)
- Two syncs completing in quick succession no longer run two overlapping Matter name refreshes and prunes (duplicate renames, "already registered" errors). (MAT-07)

## Migration notes
- MQTT consumers: retained state topics of excluded devices published by earlier versions are not cleared automatically; delete them on the broker if needed.
- No change to persisted data, UUIDs, topics or config keys.

## Documentation notes
- MQTT page / README: exclusion lists now apply to MQTT state publishing too.
- Troubleshooting: the HomeKit 149-accessory limit and its warning ("HomeKit (HAP) limit reached ...").
- ARCHITECTURE: sync lifecycle — `onSyncStarted` (each login) opens the HAP and Matter discovery cycles; end of sync = HAP prune, then single-flight Matter finalize → prune. A sync in which a previously seen discovery family reports no device skips the Matter prune (log: "[Matter] Prune skipped for this sync").
