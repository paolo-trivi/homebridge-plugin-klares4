# F1 — Lifecycle and prune, Matter + HAP

## Config keys

None added, renamed or removed.

Internal, for tests and emergencies only (not in the schema, not to document for users): the Matter registration timings are now options of the `MatterAccessoryRegistry` constructor (`timings`, type `MatterTimings`). The `KLARES4_MATTER_*` environment variables stay as their defaults and are read when the registry is built, no longer when the module is imported: `KLARES4_MATTER_REGISTER_TIMEOUT_MS`, `KLARES4_MATTER_REGISTER_POLL_MS`, `KLARES4_MATTER_REGISTER_POLL_MAX_MS`, `KLARES4_MATTER_REGISTER_RETRY_MS`, `KLARES4_MATTER_STATE_BOOTSTRAP_MS`, `KLARES4_MATTER_UNREACHABLE_GRACE_MS`, plus a new `KLARES4_MATTER_SETTLED_REGISTER_TIMEOUT_MS` (default 2000, see MAT-14 below). `KLARES4_MATTER_UNREGISTER_SETTLE_MS` and `KLARES4_MATTER_UNREGISTER_TIMEOUT_MS` belong to the topology coordinator (task F2) and are unchanged here.

## Changelog entries (2.3.0)

### Fixed
- A device deleted or renumbered on the panel is now removed from Matter (Apple Home, Alexa, Google) after three complete syncs, also across Homebridge restarts. Before, an endpoint restored from Homebridge's Matter cache stayed on the bridge forever, shown as reachable, with commands failing. (MAT-02)
- When the panel is unreachable, every Matter accessory of the plugin is marked "not responding" after 15 seconds, including those Homebridge restored from its cache: a Homebridge restart during a panel outage, or a panel that is still offline when Homebridge starts, no longer shows the last cached states as live. (MAT-03)
- Removal of HomeKit and Matter accessories follows the same rules, judged on each sync, reconnections included: an accessory counts as missing only if its kind of device (zones, outputs, scenarios, sensors...) answered in that sync, it is removed after three consecutive missed syncs, and a sync that sees fewer than half of the known accessories counts nobody as missing. A failed discovery read on reconnect no longer advances removals, and no longer keeps devices of the other kinds from being removed. (PLAT-04, MAT-02)
- The panel's own temperature probes (`sensor_system_temp_in` / `_out`) are no longer removed from HomeKit when the panel reports `NA` for them (e.g. outdoor probe without a reading at boot followed by two reconnects). (PLAT-04)
- Accessories cached by an older version for devices the exposure policy now hides (today: arming and unknown-category scenarios) are removed from HomeKit at the first sync, like excluded ones, also when the panel has only arming scenarios. (PLAT-04)
- A damaged cached accessory no longer stops Homebridge from restoring the plugin's other accessories at every start: it is reported with a warning and realigned at the next discovery. Accessories excluded in the config get no handler from the cache. (PLAT-08)
- An accessory refused by HomeKit (bridge limit of 149 accessories) no longer leaves an internal handler behind. (PLAT-02)
- Old Matter prune counters of accessories that no longer exist are cleaned from `klares4-matter-prune.json`. (MAT-17)
- On Homebridge versions whose Matter registration completes before returning (2.5), an accessory that did not come up is retried after about 2 seconds instead of 30. The plugin observes this itself and keeps the long wait where registration completes later (2.4). An endpoint now counts as up only when it answers for every cluster the plugin declared, not just one. (MAT-14)

- Matter accessories become reachable again, and the MQTT status topic reports the panel as `connected`, only when a synchronization has completed, not at login. A panel that accepts the login but refuses the data reads no longer makes cached states look live while the plugin retries. (MATTER-RC1-06)

### Changed
- The log line "[Matter] Prune skipped for this sync: no device of ... was reported (partial discovery)" is gone: a sync with a silent family now protects only that family. The Matter cycle summary reports it as `keptFamilyNotAnswered=N`. The 50% safety warning now reads "[Matter] Prune: discovery returned X of Y exposed endpoint(s), below the 50% safety floor; no endpoint counts as missing in this sync", and on HomeKit "Skipping accessory prune: discovery returned X of Y accessories (partial or failed sync)".

## Migration notes
- No persisted data changes shape: HAP cache (counter in the accessory context), `klares4-matter-prune.json` (version 1) and the Matter cache are read as before. An update from 2.2.0 loses no accessory, name or room.
- After the update, Matter endpoints of devices that are no longer on the panel (left by 2.2.0) are removed after three syncs (restarts or reconnects) in which their kind of device answered. Users who see a "Missing candidate" line for a device they still want should check that it still exists on the panel.
- HomeKit now also applies the 50% floor: a sync that discovers fewer than half of the cached accessories removes nothing (before, only a sync with zero devices was skipped).
- If the last device of a kind is deleted on the panel (e.g. the only scenario), it is not removed automatically: the plugin cannot tell an empty list from a failed read. Remove it with the matching `exclude*` list, which removes it at the first sync.

## Documentation notes
- ARCHITECTURE: "hidden by the exposure policy → treated as excluded" has one source, `DiscoveryService.isDeviceExcluded` (task A's `attachExposurePolicy`): the HAP prune removes such cached accessories at once through the registry's `isDeviceExcluded` option, so extending the policy to outputs (task C) needs no change to the prune.
- ARCHITECTURE: `platform/prune-policy.ts` holds the shared discipline (floor 50%, family guard, 3 consecutive syncs, `discoveryFamily` with the panel probes as a family each). `SyncLifecycle` collects the device IDs the panel listed in the sync (excluded ones included) and passes the answered families to both prunes; it no longer skips the Matter prune. HAP: `AccessoryRegistry.pruneStaleAccessories({ answeredFamilies })`; Matter: `MatterAccessoryRegistry.pruneStaleAccessories(sync)` judges registered endpoints and cache-restored endpoints not registered in the session in one pass (`matter-prune-tracker.ts`, counters in `matter-prune-counters.ts`).
- ARCHITECTURE: `matter-accessory-registry.ts` is the facade; the registration path is `matter-registration-flow.ts` (`resolveShape` decides the endpoint shape, `onRegistered` what a native thermostat registration clears: the two thermostat extension points). `matter-register-probe.ts` holds `RegisterSettlement`, which learns from observation whether register waits for the endpoint.
- ARCHITECTURE / Matter troubleshooting: `reachable` is published on cache ∪ registered endpoints; the platform arms the 15 s grace when it attaches the client, before the first connection. `true` (and the MQTT `panel: connected` status) is published when a sync completes, `false` (after the grace on Matter) when the connection is lost; a bare login changes nothing. On a normal reconnection the sync completes well within the grace, so the endpoints do not blink.
- Tests: the platform-level prune tests of F1 live in `test/platform-prune-epoch.test.js` (split from `platform-discovery-epoch.test.js`, which had reached the 30 s per-file budget: each reconnect costs the 1 s `reconnectInterval` minimum).
- Troubleshooting: the new log lines above; "Skipping cache handler init for X: missing or malformed device context".

## Points to verify on the field
- Real bridge after the update: endpoints of devices deleted on the panel go away after three syncs; nothing else does (watch `missingCandidates` and `keptFamilyNotAnswered` in the Matter cycle summary for the first days).
- Panel switched off with Homebridge running and Homebridge restarted during the outage: Apple Home shows "not responding" after 15 s, and the accessories come back when the panel does.
- Homebridge 2.4.x (CI job of task K): the probe keeps the 30 s budget (register does not wait there); no extra recoveries in the log.
